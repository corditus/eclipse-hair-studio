About

A small studio, on purpose.

Eclipse opened in 2016 with three chairs and a simple idea: fewer clients a day, more attention on each. We kept the room quiet and the schedule generous, because good hair takes time.

We use ammonia-free colour and low-waste products wherever we can, and we will tell you honestly when a look will not suit your hair. You should leave knowing exactly how to keep it looking this way at home.

“Come in for the cut. Stay because it still works three months later.”
